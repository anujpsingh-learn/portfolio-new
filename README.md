---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Systems

- **Frontend**: Vanilla HTML5, CSS3 Custom Properties, Modern Geometric Typography (Plus Jakarta Sans, Syne, Inter) + Tactile Accents (Patrick Hand, Caveat).
- **Animations & Interactivity**:
  - **GSAP & ScrollTrigger**: 60FPS FLIP Scroll Transition for avatar from Hero to About Me.
  - **Scroll-Drawn SVGs**: Dynamic path tracing driven via `IntersectionObserver`.
  - **Magnetic Tech Chips**: Tactile spring-physics cursor attraction on hover.
  - **Frame Scroll Preview**: Interactive vertical pan on hover & drag inside mockup screens.
- **Assets & Documents**:
  - Structured screenshots for **Managed Investment Platform**, **Huntly**, **Velo**, and **Zentro**.
  - Direct ATS-ready resume PDF download in `/Subash_Bhatta_CV/Subash_Bhatta_CV.pdf`.
